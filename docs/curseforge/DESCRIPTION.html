Better Origins

A reworked version of Origins for NeoForge 1.21.1. At the start of the game you choose an origin: each one has its own abilities, buffs and weaknesses. Origins can be changed with the Orb of Origin and customised with datapacks.

Better Origins replaces Origins (NeoForge): install one or the other, not both. It keeps the same internal ID, so datapacks and addons made for Origins (NeoForge) keep working, including addons that add new origins.

What's new compared to Origins

  • Reworked origins: Merling, Blazeborn, Elytrian, Enderian, Arachnid, Shulk, Phantom and Feline have new abilities, buffs and weaknesses.
  • 5 new origins: The Perfect Human, Superior Bat, Road Runner, Cave Worm and Angel.
  • New origin menu: origins grouped by impact (Standard, Low, Medium, High), with a coloured background for each origin, plus new icons and ability bars.
  • Custom sounds and effects for many abilities, heard by nearby players.
  • New enchantments: Sun Protection and Evolved Gills (helmets).
  • Up to 5 ability keys, all rebindable.
  • Fixes and performance: origin bonuses no longer stay after changing origin, cooldowns reset on respawn, and less network traffic and work every tick.
  • Removed origin: Avian.

Ability keys

G first ability, H second, N third, , fourth, . fifth. You can change them in Options > Controls > Key Binds.

The origins

The Human is still there for an ordinary Minecraft experience, with no abilities and no weaknesses.

Low impact

Born to run. Nothing on legs is faster, and anything in your way when you are at full speed regrets it - including walls.

Toggle for up to 30 seconds: walking +50%, sprinting 3 times faster, and you run up full blocks like slabs. Recharges while off. Uses a lot of hunger while on. Key: G

Other traits:

  • Swift Legs: You walk and sprint 20% faster.
  • Fast Metabolism: You get hungry 20% faster (300% more while Speed Burst is on).
  • Light Frame: You have 2 fewer hearts.
  • Hard Impact: Running into a wall at least 2 blocks tall (at your feet and head, or at your head and above) while sprinting with Speed Burst hurts you badly - the faster you go, the more it hurts.
  • Stunning Strike: Hitting a mob or player while sprinting with Speed Burst stuns them for 3 seconds: frozen in place and blind. They can't be stunned again for 1.5 seconds (mobs) or 5 seconds (players). The Ender Dragon is immune.

Their climbing abilities and the ability to trap their foes in spiderweb make the Arachnid perfect hunters.

You are able to climb up any kind of wall, not just ladders. Key: G (on/off)

Throw a cobweb. If it hits a creature or a player, it traps them in a real cobweb; if it hits a block, the cobweb drops and stays where it lands. 2 second cooldown. Key: H

Other traits:

  • Master of Webs: You navigate cobweb perfectly, and are able to climb in them. When you hit an enemy in melee, they get stuck in cobweb for a while. Non-arthropods stuck in cobweb will be sensed by you. You are able to craft cobweb from string.
  • Arthropod: You are an arthropod: Bane of Arthropods hurts you more.
  • Venomous Bite: Each of your hits, melee or projectile, has a 50% chance to poison the target for 3 seconds.
  • Spider Kinship: Spiders, cave spiders and spiders and centipedes from supported mods don't attack you. If you hit one, it becomes hostile towards you for 15 seconds.

Elytrians live in the wind: they glide freely and land lightly, but crashing into a wall hurts them more than others.

Every 30 seconds, you can launch yourself about 40 blocks into the air. Key: G

Throw a Breeze's wind charge where you are looking. You store up to 3 and can throw them one after another; each used charge recharges in 10 seconds, one at a time. Key: H

Other traits:

  • Winged: You have Elytra wings without needing to equip any.
  • Aerial Combatant: You deal substantially more damage while in Elytra flight.
  • Soft Landing: You take 75% less fall damage.
  • Brittle Wings: You take 50% more damage when you fly into a wall.
  • Lord of the Sky: Phantoms never attack you, not even if you hit them.

Related to Shulkers, the bodies of the Shulk are outfitted with a protective shell-like skin.

You have access to an additional 27 slots of inventory, like a small chest, which keep the items on death. Key: G

Make mobs and players within 5 blocks levitate for 3 seconds, except Shulk and pets; the area lights up purple. 8 second cooldown. Key: H

Hover in the air for up to 15 seconds: hold jump to rise, sneak to descend. The time recharges while you are not floating. Key: N

Other traits:

  • Sturdy Skin: Even without wearing armor, your skin provides natural protection.
  • Strong Arms: You are strong enough to break natural stones without using a pickaxe.
  • Unwieldy: The way your hands are formed provide no way of holding a shield upright.
  • Large Appetite: You get hungry a little faster than others.
  • Shulker Shell: Shulker bullets don't affect you.
  • Shulker Kinship: Shulkers don't attack you. If you hit one, it becomes hostile towards you for 15 seconds.
  • Heavy Body: You are 10% slower while sprinting.

Medium impact

With their cat-like appearance, the Feline scare creepers away. With the dexterity of cats, they always land safely on their feet.

Leap forcefully where you are looking, leaving a white trail behind you. 3 second cooldown, costs a little hunger. Key: G

Other traits:

  • Acrobatics: You never take fall damage, no matter from which height you fall.
  • Strong Ankles: You are able to jump higher by jumping while sprinting.
  • Velvet Paws: Your footsteps don't cause any vibrations which could otherwise be picked up by nearby lifeforms.
  • Nine Lives: You have 1 less heart of health than humans.
  • Catlike Appearance: Creepers are scared of you and will only explode if you attack them first.
  • Nocturnal: You can see well in the dark, except underwater.
  • Feline Sprint: You are 30% faster while sprinting.

Born as children of the Ender Dragon, Enderians are capable of teleporting but are vulnerable to water.

Whenever you want, you may throw an ender pearl which deals no damage, allowing you to teleport. Key: G

Other traits:

  • Hydrophobia: You receive damage over time while in contact with water.
  • Scared of Gourds: You are afraid of pumpkins. For a good reason.
  • Slender Body: You can reach blocks further away (+2.5) and creatures a bit further away (+1.5).
  • Eyes of the End: Endermen don't get angry when you look them in the eyes.
  • End Kinship: Endermen, endermites and End creatures from supported mods don't attack you. If you hit one, it becomes hostile towards you for 15 seconds.

A born miner who belongs under the stone. Underground it is fast, tough and sharp-eyed; under the open sky it feels slow and exposed.

Toggle for up to 15 seconds: mobs, players and pets within 6 blocks (not your pets, not other Cave Worms) catch parasites that grow the longer they stay: damage that ignores armour (even on undead and spiders), slowness and darkness, deadly from level 6. 5 second cooldown after switching it off. Key: G

Other traits:

  • Surface Digger: Under the open sky you mine 10% faster.
  • Deep Digger: Under a roof, or in any other dimension, you mine 35% faster.
  • Sky-Shy: Under the open sky you sprint 15% slower and lose your night vision.
  • Tunnel Runner: Underground you walk and sprint 10% faster.
  • Dark Sight: Underground you can see in the dark.
  • Exposed: Under the open sky you heal 20% slower.
  • Earth's Embrace: Underground you heal 25% faster.
  • Prospector: Everything you mine drops as if your tool had one more level of Fortune (no Fortune counts as Fortune I).
  • Soft Body: You have 1 fewer heart.

High impact

A human at the peak of their abilities: stronger, faster and tougher in everything. When death comes, Adrenaline puts them back on their feet, but then the body crashes.

When you are about to die, if you have at least 10 experience levels and Adrenaline is ready, it triggers by itself and uses 10 levels: you go back to about 5 hearts, you are invincible for 5 s, Regeneration II for 30 s; for 15 s Strength III, Speed III, Jump Boost III, full night vision, +200% swim speed and no knockback; for 30 s +50% mining speed. Then comes the crash: for 90 s you lose all bonuses (except the 11th heart). The bar shows Adrenaline in red and the crash in blue; when it is empty, Adrenaline is ready again. If you die, it is ready again right away. Triggers by itself

Other traits:

  • Strong Heart: You have 11 hearts instead of 10. This bonus is always kept.
  • Perfect Body: +20% speed, higher jump (+20%), +20% mining speed, +150% swim speed, -20% knockback, +1.5 block reach for blocks and creatures.
  • Quick Healing: All healing you receive is 10% stronger, natural regeneration included.
  • Intense Metabolism: You get hungry 25% faster.
  • Strength and Grit: Always like Strength I and Resistance I: +3 melee damage and 20% less damage taken.
  • Keen Eyes: You see better in the dark (50%).

These natural inhabitants of the ocean are not used to being out of the water for too long.

Your vision underwater is perfect. Key: G (on/off)

Other traits:

  • Gills: You can breathe underwater, but not on land.
  • Aqua Affinity: You may break blocks underwater as others do on land.
  • Fins: You swim much faster (+200%).
  • Like Water: When underwater, you do not sink to the ground unless you want to.
  • Sea Kinship: Drowned and guardians don't attack you. If you hit one, it becomes hostile towards you for 15 seconds.
  • Evolved Gills: With the Evolved Gills enchantment on your helmet you breathe longer out of water: I = 1 minute, II = 3 minutes, III = you breathe normally.

Late descendants of the Blaze, the Blazeborn are naturally immune to the perils of the Nether.

Shoot a small fireball where you are looking. 3 second cooldown. Key: G

Other traits:

  • Fire Immunity: You are immune to all types of fire damage.
  • Nether Inhabitant: Your natural spawn will be in the Nether.
  • Hydrophobia: You take damage over time while in water or rain.
  • Nether Born: You are 20% faster in the Nether (like Speed I).
  • Internal Heat: The fire inside you burns energy: you get hungry faster.
  • Nether Diet: You can eat crimson and warped fungi.
  • Fire Kinship: Blazes and magma cubes don't attack you. If you hit one, it becomes hostile towards you for 15 seconds.

A creature of the night. When the sun goes down it takes to the sky and hears every heartbeat around it, but daylight burns its skin and weakens its blows.

At night (and always outside the Overworld) double-jump to fly for up to 30 seconds; sprint while flying to go much faster. The time recharges while you are not flying. Flying costs extra hunger. If your flight ends in the air you glide down for 7 seconds. Double-jump to fly

Toggle at will: for up to 15 seconds you see the outline of every mob within 64 blocks and every player within 32 blocks, even through walls (only you see them), while your body glows with a white aura. The time recharges while it is off. Key: G

Other traits:

  • Night Eyes: You can see well in the dark.
  • Night Hunter: You are 15% faster at night (and always outside the Overworld).
  • Light Body: You take 50% less fall damage.
  • Small Frame: You have 2 fewer hearts.
  • Photoallergic: In sunlight, unless invisible, you catch fire. Sun Protection on your helmet reduces it: I = you burn half the time, II = a quarter, III = never.
  • Daylight Weakness: Under direct sunlight your attacks deal 25% less damage, even with Sun Protection.
  • Thin Wings: While flying you take 50% more damage from projectiles.

As half-human and half-phantom beings, these creatures can switch between a Phantom and a normal form.

Toggle at will: walk through solid blocks, except obsidian. You become translucent but NOT invisible. Needs more than 6 hunger and drains hunger. Key: G

Toggle at will: become invisible for up to 30 seconds. The time recharges while you are visible. Needs more than 6 hunger and drains hunger. Key: H

Other traits:

  • Fast Metabolism: Each active form makes you hungry; with both, hunger drains twice as fast.
  • Photoallergic: In sunlight, unless invisible, you catch fire. Sun Protection on your helmet reduces it: I = you burn half the time, II = a quarter, III = never.
  • Moon Armor: At night you take 20% less damage (like Resistance I).
  • Fragile: You have 8 hearts instead of 10.

A gentle guardian. It can keep its blessings for itself, share them with everyone around it, or both - but heaven's grace takes time to return.

Toggle at will: you fall 20% slower and take no fall damage. Key: N

Toggle: you get +15% speed, +20% attack speed, +15% damage, Resistance I and Regeneration I. Uses the blessing time (30 seconds). The area around you lights up. Key: G

Toggle: the 3 blessings chosen in the blessing menu go to every player and pet within 10 blocks, you included, and the area lights up. With both blessings on the time runs out twice as fast. After switching everything off you must wait 30 seconds. Key: H

Press your 4th ability key to choose which 3 blessings you give to others. Key: ,

Other traits:

  • Inner Light: You can see a little in the dark.
  • Grace: You slowly regenerate health (half a heart every 5 seconds).
  • Delicate: You have 2 fewer hearts.

Requirements

  • Minecraft 1.21.1
  • NeoForge 21.1.228 or newer
  • No other mods needed

Servers

  • Install Better Origins on the server and on every player's game.
  • Set allow-flight=true in server.properties (the Superior Bat flies).
  • Pre-generating the world (for example with Chunky) helps with fast flyers.
